The Brake Issue

The stock brakes are well balanced. But I do believe the evo/sti brakes will fit in the stock 16 inch svo rims. Also, the hubs are the same spacing as the fox so thats neat. The stock rear brake caliper piston for the svo is 54mm. 73mm on the front. The evo/irs front 2 x 40mm, 2 x 46mm rear 2 x 40mm front -> from 229.33^2mm to: 540.4^2mm rear -> from: 169.6^2mm to: 251.3^2mm
